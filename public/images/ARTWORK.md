# Classical Mediterranean artwork

These clean image assets were reconstructed with the image-generation editing tool from Paula's supplied, approved website concept. They are separate illustrations, not screenshots of the website. Headers, titles, navigation, essay cards and buttons are real HTML components.

- `mediterranean-panorama.webp` — desktop hero with extra sky and terrace so the illustrated author and the right-hand sculpture stay visible behind larger readable typography.
- `mediterranean-hero.webp` — wider original reconstruction, used for the full mobile panorama and illustrated portrait and sculpture thumbnails.
- `human-ai-symbiosis.webp` — living human and translucent anatomical robotic hand reaching towards one another in golden morning light.
- `philosophy-terrace.webp` — supporting scene of columns, books and a sailing-ship bay, adapted from the same visual reference.

Paula's illustrated likeness, auburn hair, classical burgundy clothing and contemplative profile guided the reconstruction. These are artistic reconstructions, not pixel-exact crops or claims of real photographs. For exact preservation of every facial detail, replace them with the original high-resolution text-free artwork from the designer, keeping filenames and image dimensions updated.

The generated originals remain outside the repository in `/workspace/generated_images`. WebP conversion preserves composition and reduces the total image transfer size. All assets are served locally; there are no stock-photo, font-CDN or external-image dependencies.

Author-uploaded essay covers always take precedence over fallback artwork. Category-based fallbacks are presentation only: no image URL, category, article body or other database field is overwritten.
